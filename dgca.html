DGCA + ASK Focus

  • Mental arithmetic: Quick calculation of time, speed, distance and fuel.
  • Scenario-based questions: Real-life flight situations, NOTAMs and METAR analysis.
  • TEM (Threat & Error Management): Proactive risk identification before departure.
  • Workload management: Task prioritisation during high-stress flight phases.
  • Communication: Standard ICAO/DGCA RT phraseology and cockpit briefing skills.

These are exactly the competencies airlines test during First Officer selections.
