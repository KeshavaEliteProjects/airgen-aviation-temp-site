What you study

European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) ATPL theory represents the gold standard in technical rigor. With recent regulatory updates, EASA places strong emphasis on Area 100 KSA — evaluating not just factual recall, but critical thinking and multi-crew competencies.

EASA + ASK Focus

  • Area 100 KSA preparation: Fulfilling all mandated EASA learning objectives before final authority sign-off.
  • Scenario-based assessments: Group exercises evaluating communication, workload sharing, and leadership.
  • Airline competency training: Aligning personal performance with European airline cadet selection standards.
  • TEM & CRM: Developing instincts to catch cognitive bias and automation traps early.

This aligns directly with European airline competency frameworks (e.g. Ryanair, Wizz Air, Lufthansa).

How you learn

  • Live virtual classes: Instructors holding European ATPL licences and type ratings.
  • Recorded modules: Comprehensive coverage of all 13 EASA ATPL subjects.
  • CBT practice & test series: Up-to-date European question banks (ECQB-compliant).
  • Study resources: Detailed summaries, formula cheat sheets, and chart exercises.
  • Personal mentorship: Guidance on EASA state selection (Austro Control, Transport Malta, etc.).
