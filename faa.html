What you study

US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) training is renowned worldwide for its emphasis on practical aeronautical decision-making. At AirGen, we prepare you thoroughly for FAA written exams, rigorous oral examinations, and checkrides.

Flight planningWeather briefing & METARsOral exam preparationCheckride preparationCross-country planningPerformance & weight/balance

We train you to think like an aircraft commander: evaluating real-time weather changes, calculating fuel margins, and justifying every decision to FAA Designated Pilot Examiners (DPEs).

FAA + ASK Focus

  • Real-world decision making: Single-Pilot Resource Management (SRM) and PAVE/CARE checklists.
  • Scenario training: Emergency diversion drills, engine failure glide calculations, and lost procedures.
  • Practical application: Translating FAA FAR/AIM regulations into operational cockpit habits.
  • Pilot judgement: Recognizing hazardous attitudes and applying ADM (Aeronautical Decision Making).

How you learn

  • Live virtual classes & mock orals: Rehearse checkride scenarios with certified flight instructors.
  • Recorded modules: High-definition walkthroughs of FAA airspaces, weather, and systems.
  • CBT practice & test series: Simulated FAA PAR / CAX computer exams.
  • Study resources: FAA handbooks, ACS (Airman Certification Standards) guides, and cheat sheets.
  • Personal mentorship: Guidance on US visa (M1/F1), TSA clearance, and school selection.
