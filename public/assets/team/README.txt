Put your team / faculty photos in this folder (JPG or PNG, portrait works best, about 600x860 px).
Then list them in src/utils/teamData.js, for example:
  { name: 'Capt. Your Name', role: 'Airline, aircraft or experience', tag: 'AIRGEN FACULTY', img: '/assets/team/capt-your-name.jpg' }
Leave img empty to show a placeholder silhouette.
