AIRGEN V10 CHANGES

1. White & Black button contrast
   - Primary buttons now use --accent-ink for their foreground text instead of --text-strong.
   - This fixes the monochrome/White & Black palette where black buttons previously had black text.

2. New Appearance palette
   - Added 10 · Purple & White.
   - Light white-ground UI with ultraviolet purple accent and matching aircraft key/rim lighting.
   - Includes palette tokens, scene treatment, aircraft lighting, and appearance-picker registration through the shared THEMES registry.

No existing stage data, journey transformation logic, people/aircraft animation, navigation, or hero variants were intentionally changed.
